# StudyFlow

> **An AI-powered study companion that helps students organize, understand, and master their schoolwork.**

## Overview

StudyFlow is a web app designed for **high school and college students**. Students can upload their school materials, activities, notes, and files, then use AI to turn them into useful study resources.

Instead of using multiple apps for files, reviewers, quizzes, schedules, and progress tracking, everything is managed in one place.

---

## Core Features

### 📚 Upload & Organize
Students can upload:

- PDF
- PPTX
- DOCX
- Images
- Notes
- Activities

Organize everything by subject, topic, and semester.

### 🤖 AI Study Assistant
Students can ask questions about their uploaded materials.

Examples:

- "Explain this topic."
- "Give me a hint."
- "Quiz me."
- "Why is my answer wrong?"
- "Make this easier to understand."

### 🧠 AI Reviewer Generator
Automatically generate:

- Reviewers
- Summaries
- Flashcards
- Key terms
- Important concepts
- Practice questions

### 📝 AI Quiz Generator
Generate quizzes from uploaded materials.

Supports:

- Multiple choice
- True/False
- Identification
- Short answer
- Mock exams

- Quiz scores

- Study time
